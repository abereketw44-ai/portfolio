About Me

I am an electrochemist with 5+ years of hands-on research experience in solid-state battery technology. My expertise spans solid sulfide electrolytes, halide solid electrolytes, garnet solid electrolytes, and next-generation lithium metal batteries.

Currently based in Taipei, Taiwan, I hold a Ph.D. in Electrochemistry and Advanced Battery Technology from National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (NTUST), ranked 3rd among all universities in Taiwan in technology.

My research focuses on developing moisture-stable sulfide superconductors, tuning interfacial compositions, and creating novel artificial SEI formations for lithium metal protection. My findings have been published in high-impact journals including Nano Energy, Energy Storage Materials, and ACS publications.
